# Captain paid time off (2022)

Policy POL-HR-014, effective 2022, grants captains 15 paid days off each calendar year. Paid time away from the vessel is recorded on the Saltmere crew ledger. This text is the stale version and is not the rule in force.

The 2022 copy remains in the archive so crews can see what changed. It must not be used to approve a new request.
