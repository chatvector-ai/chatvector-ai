# Captain paid time off

Policy POL-HR-014, effective 2026, grants captains 18 paid days off each calendar year. Paid time away from the vessel is recorded on the Saltmere crew ledger. Unused days do not convert to wages. The current rule replaces the 2022 text of the same policy number.

Requests go to the crew desk five days before the sailing the captain will miss. Brinewick Transit Cooperative applies this rule to captains home-ported at Saltmere.
