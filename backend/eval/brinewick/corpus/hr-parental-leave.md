# Parental leave

Policy POL-HR-031 grants twelve weeks of paid parental leave when a captain becomes a parent. Paid time away from the vessel is recorded on the Saltmere crew ledger. Those weeks are in addition to the annual paid-time-off balance in the current captain rule.

The crew desk opens the leave on the day the captain names. Brinewick Transit Cooperative does not reduce those weeks because a holiday falls inside them.
